# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want PearUI to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or opinions. We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful.
- Respect different skill levels, languages and points of view.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or images, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, trolling, or anything else that is unprofessional in a collaborative space.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and comments, and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/guruswarupa). Reports are kept confidential.
